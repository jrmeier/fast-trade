How to read this

Trend strategies

These buy strength. They performed best over the full BTC window and have the largest upside, but their recent 14-day weakness means entries should wait for confirmation instead of chasing.

Mean-reversion strategies

These wait for sharp dips. They have lower drawdown and fewer trades. They are useful when BTC pulls back while the longer trend remains intact.

Coin shortlist

The top coin list is for deciding what to model next. Liquidity is real enough for small-account trading, but volatility is high; use practical ranges, not raw model tails.
